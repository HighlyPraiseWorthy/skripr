# Skripr Voice Framework

> Canonical reference for all user-facing Skripr copy: landing page, Product Hunt, emails, in-app microcopy, onboarding, SEO pages, Reddit comments, founder posts. Read this before writing copy.

## Two voice contexts
| Context | Use for | Style |
|---|---|---|
| Community | Reddit comments, replies, founder posts | Enrique Austin base + creator-tone overlays |
| Brand/marketing | Landing, Product Hunt, emails, in-app | Linear + Beehiiv + Subscribr references |

Both share the hard rules. Context picks the blend.

## Hard rules (always, no exceptions)
- No dashes (em or en). Never.
- Short sentences. One idea per sentence. If it needs a comma, consider breaking it into two.
- Fifth-grade reading level. If a 12-year-old stumbles, rewrite.
- No SaaS-marketer words. Banned: game-changing, revolutionary, amazing, unlock, supercharge, leverage, seamless, robust, powerful, streamline, cutting-edge, next-level, elevate.
- No dated copy tropes. Banned: "blank page", "blank doc", "blank canvas", "blank prompt", "blank template", "blinking cursor". They read as 2019 copywriting. Use "from scratch", "from zero", "from nothing", or "starting cold" instead.
- No hedging. No "might," "could potentially," "I think." State it or don't.
- Validation before correction (community). Acknowledge what's right before redirecting.
- One concrete insight always. Never just agree. Add something actionable.
- Humanized, founder-in-the-trenches, not brand-manager.
- No fabricated stats, user counts, or results. Skripr is pre-traction. Use real numbers only.

## Base: Enrique Austin (community default)
Warm but direct. Validates first, corrects second. Humor to soften, never sarcastic. One concrete insight on top. Mentor in the comments, not a salesperson. Avoid: starting with "I," empty compliments ("Love this!"), feedback without acknowledgment, pitching.

## Creator-tone overlays (layer on the base by goal)
- Hormozi (blunt closer): landing hero, X, Product Hunt tagline, pricing. Bold claim, number/proof, "here's why," framework, CTA. One idea per line, white space, no adjectives.
- Naval (calm contrarian): long Reddit posts, founder story, email subjects. One counterintuitive truth, dense, aphorism-style, quotable.
- Gary Vee (documentarian): video/short-form audio ONLY. Energetic, repetition. Reads unhinged in text, so avoid in writing.
- Paul Graham (essayist): 300+ word Reddit posts, blog, SEO articles. One uncomfortable truth defended clearly, no hedging, reader as peer. Needs space, avoid for short copy.
- MrBeast (retention architect): hooks, title advice, in-app feedback. Think drop-off points. Payoff first. Tangible mechanics ("first 7 seconds") over vague advice.

## Synthesized blend (no specific channel)
Hormozi structure (short lines, bold claims, numbers) + Naval density (one truth per sentence) + Enrique warmth (validate first, peer energy) + MrBeast specificity (tangible mechanics).

## Canonical origin story (use this framing everywhere)
Confirmed by Anton 2026-07-06. Every listing, about page, founder post, and interview answer uses THIS angle, not a generic "founder noticed a pattern" version:

> Every small creator has watched a channel in their niche pull millions of views and wondered what they know that I don't. The answer is rarely luck. It is hooks, structure, and pacing that most people cannot see by just watching. Skripr makes that visible. Paste a video that is winning in your niche and it reverse-engineers the playbook behind it, then writes a script on your topic in your voice that follows the same playbook.

The emotional core: the small creator SEES the winners in their niche but cannot READ them. Skripr makes the invisible playbook visible. Do not drift back to "the founder kept watching channels blow up" framings; the pain belongs to the small creator, not the founder's observation.

## Brand references (marketing only)
- Linear: opinionated confidence, no softening.
- Beehiiv: creator-to-creator, "I built this because I needed it."
- Subscribr: outcome-obsessed, specific timeframes, numbers over adjectives.
- Lemon Squeezy: founder vulnerability, real numbers.

## Reddit rules
Target: r/NewTubers, r/SmallYTChannel, r/NewYouTubeChannels, r/PartneredYoutube.
1. Never open with Skripr. Build value first. Mention only if asked or undeniable.
2. Validate the OP first.
3. One concrete actionable insight per comment. Not a list.
4. Sound like a creator who learned it the hard way, not a founder selling.
5. No exclamation points. No emojis unless the subreddit demands it.
6. Two to three sentence blocks max. White space reads as confidence.

## Anti-patterns to reject
"Unlock the power of," "seamlessly integrates," "our AI-powered platform," "take your channel to the next level," opening a Reddit comment with a pitch, feature lists with no benefit context, any 3+ sentence block with no line break.
